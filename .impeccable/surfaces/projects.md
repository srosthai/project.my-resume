# Surface: Projects gallery

Mode: Experience

## Direction contract

THESIS: Projects are discovered through a liquid FlexCarousel — refuse the static card grid portfolio.

OWN-WORLD: Deep graphite stage (#0A0B0D to #14161A), cool chalk text (#E8E6E1), single copper accent (#C4A574) for actions only. Display: "Syne" avoided — use "Familjen Grotesk" for name/headings and "Source Serif 4" for long detail copy. Soft vignette atmosphere; no cards on the index.

STORY: Visitor meets the name, browses work as a film strip, opens one project for the written case.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed dark stage. Top-left: name + short role line. Center: FlexCarousel (~560px) of project covers with captions. Below captions: primary "View details" CTA bound to the active slide. No stats, no card grid.

FORM: Brief-pinned FlexCarousel (liquid preset) as signature interaction; code-led build.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
